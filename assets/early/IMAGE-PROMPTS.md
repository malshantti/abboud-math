# رسومات الصفين الأول والثاني

وُلّدت الصور الأربع بأداة توليد الصور المدمجة، ثم حُفظت محليًا بصيغة WebP شفافة (عرض أقصى ٥٢٠ بكسل) كي تُعرض دون اتصال. الشخصية مستندة إلى صورة زيتون المحلية `assets/img/av-read.webp` كمرجع هوية. الرسومات زخرفية لمداخل المواد؛ مشاهد الأسئلة والعدد والكسور الدقيقة ما زالت من `art.js`.

**الوصف المشترك:**

> Create one polished standalone educational app illustration for Palestinian children age 6–8. Use the attached mascot ONLY as identity reference: friendly small dark-olive green olive fruit with two leaves, big round warm eyes and black-and-white keffiyeh scarf. Draw the entire character. Bright clean 2.5D clay / soft vector style consistent with the website's grade 8 illustrated covers, controlled crisp shapes, Duolingo-like playful energy but no owl or trademark. Transparent background, square composition with ample margin. No letters, numbers, words, glyphs, flags, logos, emojis, watermark, musical instruments, or distracting extra characters. Make silhouettes legible at 180 px.

**إضافة لكل صورة:**

| الملف | وصف المشهد المضاف إلى الوصف المشترك |
|---|---|
| `math.webp` | Mascot exploring math with three large wooden colored counting blocks, a bright blue simple geometric triangle, and small red apple counters. Strong green, sky blue and orange accents. |
| `arabic.webp` | Mascot reading an open storybook beside a large warm yellow pencil and simple blooming olive branch. Do not render any writing on pages. Warm purple, green and orange accents. |
| `english.webp` | Mascot in a cheerful discovery scene with a blue school backpack, two simple blank flashcards, and a bright small globe showing abstract land. No English letters. Blue, green and yellow accents. |
| `life.webp` | Mascot investigating the natural world with a magnifying glass, small green leaf, gentle sun and water droplet. Mint green, orange and sky-blue accents. |
